Bryan's Personal Hermes Connector

This is a private, single-user integration that connects Bryan Tan's self-hosted Hermes Agent to his Google account.

It supports user-requested automations involving Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Sheets, and Docs. Examples include reading application confirmations, maintaining a private internship tracker, showing a morning calendar briefing, and adding confirmed bookings to Calendar.

The integration is not offered to the public. Access is limited to the owner's Google account and runs on the owner's private server.
