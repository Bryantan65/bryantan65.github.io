Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

This policy describes how Bryan's Personal Hermes Connector accesses and uses Google user data. The connector is a private, single-user integration operated by Bryan Tan on a self-hosted server.

Google data accessed

When authorized by the owner, the connector may access Gmail messages and metadata; Google Calendar events; Drive files and metadata; Contacts; Google Sheets; and Google Docs. It may create or modify emails, calendar events, spreadsheets, documents, and Drive content only when required by an owner-requested automation.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide features requested by the owner. These include retrieving relevant email, showing calendar information, synchronizing confirmed bookings, updating private trackers, and performing other explicitly requested Google Workspace actions.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and automation state are stored on the owner's private server and are not published in this website or its source repository. Generated summaries and task history may be stored locally to support the connector's operation, auditing, and duplicate prevention. Access to the server and credentials is restricted to the owner.

AI processing and service providers

When the owner asks the connector to interpret or summarize content, the minimum relevant content may be processed by the AI model provider configured by the owner. Google APIs and the configured AI provider process data as service providers needed to complete the requested task. The connector does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with unrelated third parties. It is disclosed only to infrastructure and AI service providers necessary to perform an owner-requested operation, or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only for as long as needed for the requested automation, operational history, security, or duplicate prevention. The owner may delete local data at any time and may revoke the connector's Google access from the Google Account permissions page.

Limited use

The connector's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests may be sent to bryantan65@gmail.com.
